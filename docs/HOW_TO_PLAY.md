# How to play BLACKVEIN

A quick guide to your first hour.

## The first ten minutes

1. **Follow the GOAL line** under your health bar. The first few goals teach the basics and pay XP.
2. **Sprint (Shift / RT) straight into a building** to run up it. At the top you vault onto the roof.
3. **Hold jump** to charge a super jump. Jump off a roof and land hard for a shockwave.
4. **Consume someone (F / LB).** It heals you, refills biomass and gives you their disguise.
5. **Open Skills (Tab / Back)** whenever you level up. Good first picks: **Glide**, **Air Dash**,
   **Claws**, **Thermal Vision**, **Dive Bomb**.

## Staying alive

- The bar under the minimap shows how the military sees you:
  - **HIDDEN** — walk calmly and they ignore you. A yellow bar fills while they get suspicious.
  - **ALERT** — they're hunting you. The diamonds are *heat*: more heat brings rocket teams,
    tanks, gunships and airstrikes.
  - **SEARCHING** — you broke line of sight. Stay out of view until they give up.
- **The fastest escape:** get out of sight and **consume someone**. A new disguise makes the
  military lose you instantly.
- Health regenerates when you avoid damage for a few seconds. Consuming heals much faster.
- **Shield** (blocks bullets from the front) and **Armor** (take less damage) unlock around
  levels 6–8. Press X / D-pad up to switch.

## Getting stronger

- XP comes from **kills, consumes, destroyed vehicles, combos, world events, goals and achievements**.
- **World events** pop up every minute or two — follow the colored diamonds on the compass and
  minimap. They pay the most XP.
- **Long combos** (x8 and up) pay bonus XP. Keep hitting without stopping.
- Every level gives a skill point; every 5th level gives two.

## Places and people worth knowing

- **The military base** (south-west, walled compound): consume the **commander** to unlock
  **Artillery Strike**. Walk in disguised as a soldier and they won't suspect you.
- **High-Value Targets** (scientists with escorts) carry **memories**. Collect 5 different
  memories and reach level 18 to unlock the finale.
- **Hunters** leap across rooftops after you. Fight them in the open.
- **Bosses**: the Leviathan (level 10+), the Juggernaut (level 12+), and Patient Zero (the finale).

## Tips

- Grab a car (E / RB) and throw it at a tank. Musclemass throws harder.
- Tether (C / LT) at a helicopter to latch on and hijack it.
- Thermal vision helps at night and in fog.
- The Biomass Decoy draws every gun in the area — great for slipping away.
- Settings has mouse sensitivity, field of view, key rebinding, crowd density and graphics quality.
